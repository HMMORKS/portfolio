Final Year Project: Design, Development and Implementation of Portable Capacitive Electromagnetic Flowmeter

Designed and developed a portable capacitive electromagnetic flowmeter focused on non-invasive fluid flow measurement. The project integrates capacitive sensing, signal conditioning, and embedded systems to detect and process flow-related signals for real-time measurement. It explores the application of electromagnetic flow measurement principles and capacitive sensing techniques to develop a practical, compact, and portable solution. The project was carried out under the supervision of Dr. Mojeeb bin Ihsan, Director of MERL Pakistan, combining instrumentation, electronics, and embedded system design.
